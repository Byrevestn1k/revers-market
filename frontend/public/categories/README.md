# Category illustrations

`category-atlas.png` is an original image generated with the built-in ImageGen tool.
The 5 × 4 atlas is used directly by CSS, without copying OLX artwork.
Subcategories inherit their main category's illustration. Tile indices 18–19 are reserved.

Generation prompt:

> Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: ONE cohesive category illustration atlas bitmap for Ukrainian community marketplace ДещоТреба. Create exactly a 5-column by 4-row grid, equal-sized square cells, entire image aspect ratio 5:4, preferably 2000x1600. Each cell is a distinct original delightful small clay 3D still-life illustration centered with generous padding, consistent scale, mint green / teal with warm terracotta and honey accents, soft natural shadows, plain very pale warm ivory background identical in all cells. No grid lines, no text, no labels, no logos, no branding. Do not imitate OLX category pictures. Subjects in strict row-major order: row1 [wooden toy stacking rings with tiny teddy bear; cozy architectural house miniature; compact sage green car; steel gear and wrench; work briefcase with notebook]; row2 [friendly sitting corgi with cat; comfortable armchair with potted plant; laptop with smartphone; toolbox with service bell; cozy bed with pillow and moon]; row3 [drill and rental key tag; terracotta jacket on hanger with sneaker; bicycle wheel with ball and guitar; open gift box with heart; two curved exchange arrows around small parcels]; row4 [vintage star-shaped collectible trophy; game controller and headphones; basket of wheat stalks and vegetables; reusable shopping bag with simple unmarked parcel; potted sprout]. All twenty cell subjects must be clearly separate, do not overlap boundaries. This atlas will be used directly by CSS as twenty equal 20%-width and 25%-height tiles. Consistent clean square framing in each cell is essential. Original illustrations, readable at 80px, high quality.
